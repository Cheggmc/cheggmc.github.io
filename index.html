WHAT IS CHEGG

Chess rules, Minecraft pieces, your own deck.

Each match plays out on a 10×8 checkerboard with a red spawn zone on one end and a blue one on the other. Every minion moves, attacks, and dashes differently — and you decide which 15 go into your deck.

Build a 15-card deck

Fill you deck with 15 minions of your choosing, then play a match. You draw one minion per turn.

Spend your mana wisely

Mana climbs from 1 to a cap of 6, one point per turn, and resets every turn. Spend it spawning minions, attacking, dashing, or firing off unique abilities.

Protect your Villager

Your Villager is your king, place it anywhere in your spawn zone and defend it. Eliminate the enemy Villager first and you win the match.
