own screenshots / textures without me redistributing anyone else's assets).
Save each one into the `src/` folder using the **exact filename** below.

Easiest source for the 18 spawn-egg icons: take your own screenshots from
in-game (right-click each spawn egg in your inventory and screenshot it,
then crop to a square), or export the item icons from your resource pack.
All 18 cards render fine even before the images exist — the item-frame slot
will just sit empty until you add the file.

Nothing else in the site needs an image; the checkerboard patterns,
hero background, and minion move/attack previews are all drawn live with
CSS/JS, no image files required.
