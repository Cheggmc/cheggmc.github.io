01The Basics

Chegg is a turn-based, deck-building strategy game — originally designed to be played live inside Minecraft, and now fully automated on this server. Minions are represented by spawn eggs, drawn from a personal deck, and placed onto a checkerboard-style arena.

The game is built to be adaptable. Beyond the roster covered here, players are encouraged to dream up their own minions and house rules with their opponent.

02The Board

Chegg is played on a 10×8 checkerboard. The two back rows on either end are colored — one red, one blue — and mark each player's spawn zone.

Spawn zones are the only place you can place new minions. Once a minion is on the board, it can move anywhere the rules allow.

The middle of the board can be customized by mutual agreement between players — see Arena Mods below for ideas the server supports.

03The Goal

At the start of every match, each player places their Villager somewhere inside their spawn zone. The Villager functions like a King in chess — the goal of the match is to eliminate the opponent's Villager. Whoever does it first wins.

04Deck Building

A deck is 15 minions of your choosing. At the start of a match, your deck is shuffled and you draw from it one minion at a time.

Start of the matchEach player draws 3 minions into their opening hand

05Mana

Mana is the currency behind everything you do on your turn — spawning minions, attacking, dashing, and some unique abilities. Your mana pool ranges from 0 to a cap of 6.

06Spending Mana

There are three core ways to spend mana:

Spawning. Every minion has a mana cost, shown on its card. Spawn it from your hand onto any open square in your spawn zone.

Attacking. Attacking always costs 1 mana, no matter which minion is attacking — the range and shape of the attack depends entirely on the minion. As an example: a Skeleton can strike diagonally up to 3 squares away, so for a single mana it can eliminate an enemy sitting on that diagonal.

Each minion can only attack once per turn — no matter how much mana you're willing to spend.

07Dashing & Timing

Dashing. Every minion on the board gets 1 free move per turn. After that free move is used, it can move again for 1 mana — that's a dash. A minion cannot dash and attack in the same turn, and it can't move again after attacking, even with its free move still unused.

A minion's dash usually extends its normal move pattern further in the same directions.

New minions sit out their first turn. A freshly spawned minion cannot move, attack, dash, or use an ability until the turn after it's placed.

Friendly fire is real for area attacks like the Creeper and Puffer-Fish — double-check what's standing near your target before you commit.

08Arena Mods

Beyond the standard checkerboard, boards can be modified by mutual agreement — a river splitting the middle with a single bridge, walls, natural barriers, lava, or hazards that push minions around. Certain minions, like the Puffer-Fish or Phantom, make natural sense crossing terrain other minions can't.

House rules like these are baked into the spirit of Chegg — feel free to agree on your own arena twists with your opponent before a match.
